I’m excited to share a new update we’ve been working on at Speechify.

We just launched Voice Typing inside the Speechify Chrome Extension. You can now speak naturally, and Speechify will type for you anywhere online: Google Docs, Gmail, forms, chats, anything. It also cleans up filler words and tidies the text, so you can focus on the idea instead of the keyboard.
We also added Talk to Speechify. This lets you ask questions about any webpage you’re reading. Speechify first reads the page, then you can say things like:
 • “Explain this in simple words.”
 • “Summarize this section.”
 • “How should I reply to this email?”

I’ve been testing both features in my own workflow, and they save a surprising amount of time. It feels like having a small assistant built right into the browser — one that reads, types, and helps you think through what’s in front of you.

If you want to try it, here’s the Chrome Extension:
