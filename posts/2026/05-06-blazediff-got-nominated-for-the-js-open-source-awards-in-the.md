BlazeDiff got nominated for the JS Open Source Awards in the Most Exciting Use of Technology category.

Spent the morning going through the other nominees, and two of them feel like glimpses of where the web is heading.

TypeGPU lets you write WebGPU shaders in TypeScript with real type inference. WebGPU is already the most underrated thing shipping in browsers right now, and this makes it actually approachable for people who don't live in graphics code.

ForesightJS predicts where your cursor is going before it gets there, so you can prefetch or preload based on intent. Small idea, but the kind of thing that quietly changes how snappy an app feels.

The fourth nominee is TanStack DB from Tanner Linsley, which you probably already know if you've touched TanStack Query. Reactive client store, live queries, the usual high bar from that ecosystem.

Being in a lineup like that is the part that doesn't quite feel real. Voting opens before JSNation in June, link in the comments if you want to look through the category.
