Uber just capped Claude Code at $1,500 a month per engineer.

Same cap on Cursor, with a separate budget for each tool. That is not a cost-control memo. It is per-seat software breaking in public.

Flat seats worked when a seat cost the vendor almost nothing. A token-metered seat has no ceiling. One engineer running agents all day burns what ten light users pay combined. The vendor eats that gap or caps it. Uber capped it.

I run my entire writing pipeline on Claude Code, agents calling agents. You hit the usage wall fast enough to feel where this goes. The bill scales with how hard each person works, not how many people you hire.

So stop budgeting AI tools like SaaS seats. Budget them like cloud compute. Uber's cap is your next planning cycle, not their weird policy.
