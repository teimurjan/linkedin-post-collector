You haven't forgotten how to code. You've forgotten how to be stuck.

That bearblog post climbing HN this week (479 points, 420 comments) is a room full of engineers watching their skills erode and naming the wrong one. The author mourns debugging: a bug that once ate a full day now gets one-shotted by Claude Code. All true. But the lost day was never the cost.

I run my whole posting pipeline through Claude Code. Research, drafts, analytics, the entire loop. What went soft wasn't writing code. It was the tolerance for sitting in an unsolved problem long enough to actually understand it.

That tolerance is the only thing that ever built senior judgment. You earned it by being stuck for two days and refusing to tab away. The agent removes the friction. The friction was the teacher.

Keep one hard problem a week you solve without it. Not for nostalgia. For the judgment.
