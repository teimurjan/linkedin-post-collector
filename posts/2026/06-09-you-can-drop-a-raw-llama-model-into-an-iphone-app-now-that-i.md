You can drop a raw Llama model into an iPhone app now.

That is what Apple actually shipped at WWDC. It retired Core ML, the on-device ML framework it has run since 2017, and replaced it with Core AI. One Swift API. Load your own weights, Llama or Mistral, with no converting everything to Apple's format first. The system routes each call on-device or to the cloud for you.

Everyone covered the wrong headline. Siri running on Gemini got the clicks. This is the bigger shift.

For nine years Apple forced every model through its own format and its own runtime. The most closed AI stack in tech just made itself model-agnostic.

If you ship iOS apps, this is the part of the keynote to rewatch. The plumbing you used to hand-roll is now the platform.
