Curl is closing its security inbox for all of July.

No HackerOne reports, no security email, for a full month. Curl is the most-deployed C library on earth. It sits in your phone, your car, your router. And the maintainers decided the safest move is to stop listening.

Think about that. The fix for a security process under load is to turn reporting off.

This is what AI did to open source security. Not more bugs. It broke the economics of disclosure. Stenberg has spent years documenting AI-generated reports that read like the real thing, waste a day each, and bury the one that mattered. When anyone can mint a plausible vuln in seconds, the human reviewer becomes the bottleneck, and the bottleneck burns out.

The only lever left is to rate-limit humanity to survive the bots.

Your dependencies are maintained by people. The people are tired.
