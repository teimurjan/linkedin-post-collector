A job application goes in. Nothing comes back.

The screener that rejected you scored one resume 90, then 74, then 88.

HackerRank open-sourced that screener. The same resume, a hundred runs, scores from 66 to 99. One drop of 16 points came from deleting a few debug print lines. Nothing else changed.

It is an LLM grading you out of 100. Drop the temperature, run it again, the noise stays. You cannot tune this away. Ask a model whether your projects are worth 18 points or 24 and the answer changes every time you ask.

A gate that cannot reproduce its own score is not measuring you. If the cutoff is 85, a strong resume fails most of the time. That is not filtering for quality. It is just filtering.
