For two months, a top-3 OpenRouter model hid its own name.

It went by Owl Alpha, ran in real agentic workflows, and reached the top three by call volume. Nobody realized what it was.

This week Meituan said it was theirs. Yes, the food delivery company. It is LongCat-2.0, 1.6 trillion parameters, trained end to end on 50,000 Huawei chips with no NVIDIA involved. It scores 59.5 on SWE-bench Pro, beating GPT-5.5 and just behind Opus 4.7, at around a twentieth of the cost.

What gets me is the order it happened in. Everyone who rated it had already decided it was good. The objections only started once the name was on it.

So separate the two questions. Is the output any good, and do you trust a model trained in China on Chinese chips. Both are fair. We just keep using the second one to answer the first.

Would you have scored it lower if the label came first? Be honest with that one.
