Top-tier realtime voice is not a premium feature anymore.

Voice Arena scores voice models with blind native-speaker panels and publishes the confidence intervals. This week a streaming model launched and debuted straight into a statistical tie for the top of the realtime field. The only model clearly ahead does not stream, so it is out for a live agent.

It did that on day one, with eight voices, English only, on a fraction of the votes. And it runs at six dollars per million characters. For anyone building a live voice agent, the quality bar you would ship to a user is no longer the expensive tier.

Disclosure: the model is Speechify's Simba 3.2, so weigh me accordingly. It is days old on one eval. Blind panels do not know who is cheap. Go run the same line through the board and listen.
