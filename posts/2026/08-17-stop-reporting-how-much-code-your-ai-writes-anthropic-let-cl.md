Stop reporting how much code your AI writes.

Anthropic let Claude Code run unsupervised on its own apps for weeks. It opened 388 pull requests across iOS, Android, desktop, web, and the CLI. Reviewers merged 180. That is a 46% merge rate, and it is the only public one I have seen.

Paisabazaar's CTO said today that AI now writes 86% of their new code. Generation numbers have been easy for two years. They tell you what the agent produced, not what your team accepted.

The writeup never says what the 208 rejected pull requests cost in review time. Thirty seconds each and 46% is a great deal. If each one needs a real read, the work just moved to your reviewers.

Ask your team for the merge rate. If nobody has it, you don't have a productivity number yet.
