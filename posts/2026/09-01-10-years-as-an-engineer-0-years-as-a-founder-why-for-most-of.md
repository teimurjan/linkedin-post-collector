10 years as an engineer. 0 years as a founder.

Why? For most of my career, I got used to executing a plan and thinking strategically only at the tech level.

That changed when I joined Growth at Speechify. For the first time, my engineering work was directly connected to the business side, and a lot of things clicked. Most importantly: I realized I could run a business on my own.

So I started small and iterate fast, building from my own ideas about things I miss.
Today I'm happy to share that one of those apps, https://waitprofessor.com/, has its first paying active user.

One user. But it's the difference between an idea and a business.

From here on I'm shifting my posting angle a bit: how I run apps with zero headcount. Every function that would normally be a hire, built instead.
