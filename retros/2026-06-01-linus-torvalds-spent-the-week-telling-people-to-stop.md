## Repeat, modify, or block?

**Modify** — repeat the craft, recalibrate the expectation.

The reach is real but it is almost certainly topic-driven, not wedge-driven. A named authority figure (Torvalds) plus a universal developer anxiety (AI in my codebase) plus a take that is contrarian to the hype yet flattering to the reader ("you, the careful one, are right") is a formula that escapes the follower graph into broad distribution. The engagement rate confirms it: 0.2% likes and 8 comments on 111k impressions is what low-intent, non-follower reach looks like. A post that deeply resonated with a core builder audience would show a far higher like and comment ratio at a fraction of the reach.

**Repeat (one concrete thing):** the shape. A front-loaded named-authority hook, a one-line firsthand credibility anchor ("I maintain a small open-source library, here is my actual rule"), and a compressed "here's the part everyone skips" inverse-signal wedge, all at ~130 words. The shorter format did not hurt reach and made the post trivially skimmable and shareable.

**Modify (one concrete thing):** stop treating impressions as the success metric for this lane. 111k is an outlier riding a famous name; do not set it as a baseline or the next post will read as a miss. For builder-depth goals, judge these news-takes on like/comment/save rate and follows, not raw reach. If the goal is discussion, the next one needs a sharper, more divisive stake to convert reach into a thread, not just a take readers nod at and scroll past.

**Do not block.** Nothing here failed. The only risk is misreading a distribution lottery as a repeatable formula.
