## Reach

750 impressions against a 443 corpus median — technically a beat, but a thin
one. The peer comparison is the real verdict: the `ai` family median sits at
56021 and `news` at 3082. This landed below both. Same family, same source
type, one-fortieth the reach of TabFM six days earlier.

What did not fail was engagement. 14 likes and 3 comments on 750 impressions is
a 1.9% like rate, the highest ratio in the recent corpus — TabFM's record post
managed 0.4%. Everyone who saw this responded to it. Almost nobody saw it.

That is a distribution outcome, not a writing outcome.

## Discussion

Two of three comments landed on the intended wedge. Carlos L. restated it
almost verbatim — "once top tier quality stops being locked behind a high
price, it changes what smaller teams are willing to build on top of it" — which
is the opinion_wedge with the audience filled in. Ismail Aminu Said extended it
to startups shipping realtime apps. The angle was received exactly as aimed.

The hook matched the body cleanly: the claim was that premium voice quality is
no longer the expensive tier, and the body produced a leaderboard position, a
streaming constraint, and a price per million characters.

## Decision: modify

The failure is topic reach ceiling, not execution. Realtime TTS evals have a
small standing audience on LinkedIn; a sharp wedge cannot conjure a room that
is not there. The two posts that cleared 30k in this window were about
dependency policy and rewrite economics — things every builder already has an
opinion about.

The vendor disclosure was the right call and did not visibly cost anything —
the on-angle comments came after it. Keep it.

**Repeat:** the disclosure-then-invite close ("weigh me accordingly … go run
the same line through the board"). It converted skeptical framing into
engagement instead of suppressing it.

**Modify:** stop spending a slot on a vendor-category benchmark unless the
wedge generalizes past the category. "Top-tier X is no longer the premium tier"
is a strong frame — apply it where the standing audience is larger than the
people who ship voice agents.
