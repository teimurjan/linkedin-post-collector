# Cursor built a GitHub competitor that still runs on GitHub

100,064 impressions against a 2-to-7-day cohort median of 876 — 114x. Second-largest
post in the corpus, behind the Linus/AI-patches post (128,280) and ahead of TabFM
(108,960). Inside the t2 band (5,154–128,280) at the top end.

## The tier call held

Pre-scored `reach_ceiling: 2`, `reach_tier: t2-universal`, on the subject *GitHub*.
That is the cleanest possible t2: every reader has used it, fought with it, and has an
opinion about it before the post exists. The score was argued from the subject, not from
the news cycle — and the news cycle was loud (HN 542/400, a same-day outage), which is
exactly the condition under which the heat-is-not-size rule usually gets violated.

This is the third prospectively tier-scored post and the second to hold. `experience_hook:
none`. That is now two t2 posts (SQLite at 6,565, this one at 100,064) that reached
without any firsthand signal, which further discredits the news-without-firsthand flag
already listed as tested-and-discredited in `post-patterns`.

## The comments validated the wedge, and corrected it

27 comments, and they landed on the intended argument rather than beside it. Remo K.
contested "none is a replacement" by name. Michael Cochran and Matthew Adams argued the
self-hosted path (GitLab CE, Forgejo). Nelson Spence read it as rip-and-replace. Atharva
Pandey argued scale. Steffen Rudkjøbing pitched a European host. Every one of those is a
response to "the lock-in is the network, not the storage" — the wedge was the thing being
discussed.

Charlie Punchatz caught a real imprecision: Origin does support native repositories where
Origin is the source of truth, and a GitHub mirror can be detached into one. The hook's
"still runs on GitHub" describes the *recommended migration path*, not Origin's
architecture. The claim survived, but it was overstated, and it cost a public concession.

That is the one thing to fix. On a t2 subject the room contains people who have read the
vendor's docs, and a hook built on a product detail has to survive them. This one nearly
didn't. The reach was not harmed — the correction thread is some of the best engagement on
the post — but the credibility ledger is separate from the reach ledger.

## Decision: repeat

Repeat the shape: a t2 subject, a falsifiable structural claim about two named actors,
no firsthand signal required. Modify one thing — verify the product detail the hook rests
on against the vendor's own documentation before publishing, not after a commenter does it.
